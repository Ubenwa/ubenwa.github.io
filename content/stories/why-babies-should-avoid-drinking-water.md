You may be curious about when and why babies should avoid drinking water. This guideline is crucial for ensuring your baby's health and well-being. Here’s a detailed look at why water is not recommended for infants under six months of age.
<!-- app-cta -->
## The Risk of Water Intoxication in Babies

One significant reason babies should not drink water is the risk of water intoxication. Infants have immature kidneys that cannot handle large volumes of water. When a baby consumes too much water, it can dilute the sodium levels in their bloodstream, leading to hyponatremia. This condition can cause symptoms such as irritability, swelling, and in severe cases, seizures. Water intoxication can be dangerous and potentially life-threatening for infants.

## Disruption of Nutritional Balance

Another reason to avoid giving water to babies is that it can disrupt their nutritional balance. Babies under six months get all the hydration they need from breast milk or formula. These fluids provide the right balance of electrolytes, nutrients, and calories essential for healthy growth. Introducing water can fill up your baby's small stomach, reducing their appetite for nutrient-dense breast milk or formula, which may lead to inadequate nutrition. Maintaining proper nutrition is critical during these early months.

By understanding why babies should avoid drinking water, you can ensure that your baby's hydration and nutritional needs are met safely and effectively.
