<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/gift-nanni-1.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: give the gift of Nanni AI" width="252">

Want to be the hero of the baby shower? Gift a lifetime subscription to Nanni AI, the leading app using cutting-edge tech to interpret babies’ cries accurately. It offers personalized soothing methods and monitors sleep patterns, diaper changes, feeding schedules, and developmental milestones effortlessly. Especially ideal for new parents! Discover this awesome gift [here](https://store.ubenwa.ai/?utm_source=internal&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=BabyShowerGifts).

## Health-focused, high-tech baby monitors

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-13-at-1-05-09-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: Nanit pro" width="227">

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-13-at-1-06-03-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: Owlet Duo" width="278">

Elevate their baby monitoring experience with the [Nanit](http://nanit.com) Pro (with Breathing wear and Smart sheets) or [Owlet](https://www.owletcare.ca/) Dream Duo (Sock and Cam). Nanit provides non-invasive breathing monitoring and growth tracking, while Owlet monitors sleep quality, heart rate, and oxygen levels. Since these options go beyond the norm, they're perfect for exceeding baby monitor expectations.

## Food delivery services

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-24-at-3-44-40-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: Uber Eats food delivery service" width="205">

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-24-at-3-44-56-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: DoorDash food delivery service" width="216">

Help ease the newborn care burden with food delivery services like [Uber Eats](http://www.ubereats.com) or [DoorDash](http://www.doordash.com). Perfect for parents managing frequent feedings and sleepless nights, this practical gift is sure to be a lifesaver in those exhausting first weeks. So gift them the freedom to ditch meal prep, cooking, and clean-up... if only for a moment!

## Professional cleaning services

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-24-at-3-47-42-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: professional cleaning service" width="337">

Give the gift of clean with professional cleaning services. Ideal for parents juggling newborn care and sleep deprivation, this thoughtful gesture takes a significant chore off their hands. So give them some time back! Simply search for cleaning services in their area to arrange this original gift.

## Smart cribs

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-24-at-3-48-43-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: Snoo smart crib" width="300">

<img src="/stories/images/unique-baby-shower-gift-ideas/screenshot-2024-07-13-at-1-40-11-pm.png" alt="Unique baby shower gift idea: Cradlewise smart crib" width="321">

Discover the future of cribs with smart technology that sets new standards. The [Cradlewise Smart Crib](https://cradlewise.com/) and [Snoo Smart Sleeper Bassinet](https://www.happiestbaby.com/products/snoo-smart-bassinet) are impressive choices. While Cradlewise features AI technology and monitors baby’s movements for customized soothing, the Snoo gently responds to fussiness with rocking and white noise, ensuring safe and peaceful sleep. But be informed... this unique baby shower gift does lean on the more expensive side.
