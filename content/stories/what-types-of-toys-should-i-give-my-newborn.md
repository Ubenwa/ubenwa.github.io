New parents, but also extended family and friends of newborns often ask “what types of toys should I give my newborn?”. Selecting the right toys for a newborn baby under 3 months of age is crucial for their early development. At this stage, infants are just beginning to explore their senses and develop basic motor skills. Before buying any toy for babies, we recommend following these general safety tips from [Kids Health here](https://kidshealth.org/en/parents/products-toys.html). When purchasing toys, **always follow the manufacturer's age recommendations.** Here’s a guide to the types of toys that are most beneficial for babies in this age range.
<!-- app-cta -->
## Sensory-Stimulating Toys

For newborn babies under 3 months, sensory stimulation is key to their development. Toys that engage multiple senses—sight, sound, and touch—are particularly valuable.

1. **High-Contrast Toys:** Newborns have limited vision and are drawn to high-contrast patterns. Toys featuring bold black-and-white designs can help stimulate their visual development. High-contrast toys are effective in capturing a baby’s attention and promoting visual tracking skills. Check out our [**Infant Development series**](/stories/category/infant-development/) for more information on newborn vision.
2. **Soft Rattles and Crinkle Toys:** Toys that make gentle sounds, such as soft rattles or crinkle toys, are excellent for auditory stimulation. The sounds help develop your baby’s hearing and can soothe them when they are fussy. Ensure these toys are lightweight and easy for tiny hands to grasp.
3. **Soft Plush Toys:** Plush toys made from soft, baby-safe materials offer comfort and tactile stimulation. They help babies begin to explore textures and can provide a sense of security. Be sure the plush toys are free from small parts and made from non-toxic materials.

## Developmentally Appropriate Toys

At this early stage, toys should also support the development of basic motor skills and encourage interaction.

1. **Tummy Time Mats:** Tummy time is crucial for strengthening your baby’s neck and shoulder muscles. Mats with built-in textures or removable toys can make tummy time more engaging and enjoyable. Tummy time is recommended as a way to promote motor development and prevent the development of flat head syndrome.
2. **Mobiles and Play Gyms:** Hanging mobiles or play gyms with dangling toys can encourage your baby to reach and grasp. These toys help develop hand-eye coordination and visual tracking skills. Ensure that any toys used in these setups are securely attached and made of safe materials.
3. **Mirror Toys:** Babies are fascinated by their own reflections. Soft, baby-safe mirror toys can encourage visual exploration and self-recognition. They also support cognitive development as babies start to understand cause and effect.

So, next time you find yourself wondering "What types of toys should I give my newborn?", check back here. For newborn babies under 3 months, choose toys that stimulate their senses and support their early development. High-contrast toys, sensory-stimulating rattles, and safe plush toys can enhance their sensory experiences. Additionally, tummy time mats, play gyms, and mirror toys promote motor skills and interaction. Always ensure that toys are age-appropriate and safe for your baby.
