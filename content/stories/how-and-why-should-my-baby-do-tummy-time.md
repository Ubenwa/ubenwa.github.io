You’ve probably heard that tummy time is good for babies, but maybe you’re still asking yourself “how and why should my baby do tummy time?”. Tummy time is an essential part of your baby’s early development. It involves placing your baby on their stomach while they are awake and supervised. This simple practice can significantly impact their physical and cognitive growth. Here’s a guide on why tummy time is important and how to incorporate it into your baby’s routine.
<!-- app-cta -->
## The Importance of Tummy Time

Tummy time offers numerous developmental benefits for infants. This practice helps strengthen the muscles in your baby’s neck, shoulders, and back. These muscles are crucial for achieving developmental milestones such as rolling over, sitting up, and crawling.

1. **Muscle Development:** When your baby spends time on their stomach, they use their muscles to lift their head, push up, and eventually crawl. This muscle strengthening is vital for motor skills development and helps prevent conditions such as flat head syndrome (plagiocephaly) by promoting healthy head and neck positioning.
2. **Visual and Cognitive Stimulation:** Tummy time also provides an opportunity for your baby to engage with their environment from a different perspective. This change in viewpoint can stimulate visual tracking and cognitive development as your baby interacts with toys and faces.
3. **Reduced Risk of Developmental Delays:** Regular tummy time helps prevent delays in motor skills development. It is recommended to incorporate tummy time into your baby’s daily routine to ensure they meet developmental milestones on time.

## How to Do Tummy Time

Incorporating tummy time into your baby’s routine can be easy and enjoyable with the right approach:

1. **Start Slow:** Begin with short sessions of tummy time, about 2-3 minutes, several times a day. As your baby gets accustomed to it, gradually increase the duration. Aim for a total of 20-30 minutes of tummy time each day by the time your baby is 3 months old.
2. **Use a Comfortable Surface:** Place your baby on a clean, soft surface such as a play mat or blanket. Ensure that the area is safe and free from hazards. A tummy time mat with bright colors and textures can also make the experience more engaging.
3. **Engage and Encourage:** Get down on the floor with your baby to make tummy time more interactive. Use toys, mirrors, or your own face to encourage your baby to lift their head and reach out. Singing, talking, or playing with your baby can make tummy time more enjoyable and stimulating.
4. **Incorporate Tummy Time into Daily Routines:** Try to include tummy time after diaper changes or naps when your baby is calm and alert. Consistency is key to making tummy time a natural part of your baby’s day.

Tummy time is a crucial practice for your baby’s physical and cognitive development. By starting with short sessions and gradually increasing the time, you can help your baby build strength, improve motor skills, and engage with their environment. Consistent tummy time is essential for healthy development and achieving key milestones. By incorporating tummy time into your baby’s daily routine, you support their growth and development in a simple yet effective way.
